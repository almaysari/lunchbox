House Rules of the Kitchen

Read this page first. These rules apply to every recipe. · قواعد المطبخ

⚠️ Einas (Madam) – ALWAYS

  • NO spicy food. No chili, no hot sauce, no "medium". Mild only.
  • NO regular milk or cream. Use OAT milk or lactose-free milk/cheese only.
  • NO beans / legumes (lobia, chickpeas, lentils, foul).
  • NO seafood or fish.
  • Chicken is her meat. Lamb or beef dishes: cook her a chicken version or a salad with grilled chicken.
  • She is pregnant: food must be fully cooked (no pink chicken, no runny eggs), fresh, and clean.
Tagalog: Walang maanghang, walang regular na gatas, walang beans, walang isda. Manok lang. Lutong-luto lahat.

👨‍💼 Mohamed (Sir)

  • All cuisines. Chicken, lamb and beef.
  • Does NOT eat: foul (fava beans), mujaddara, seafood.
  • Likes: fresh juice, fresh fruit. No bread with breakfast.
  • Spice level: mild to medium. Chili sauce on the side, never mixed in.

📏 Quantities

  • Every table has two columns: 1 person and 2 persons. Use the column that was requested that day.
  • 1 cup = 250 ml. 1 tbsp = 15 ml. 1 tsp = 5 ml. Use the measuring cups and spoons in the drawer, not a random glass.
  • Rice: always wash 3 times until the water is clear, then soak 20-30 min.
  • Dinner portions are half of lunch portions unless told otherwise.
Gamitin ang measuring cup at kutsara. Huwag tantiyahin.

🔥 Cooking basics

  • Onion "golden": medium heat 8-12 min, stir often. Brown = good. Black = throw away and start again.
  • Chicken is cooked when juices run CLEAR and no pink near the bone (74 °C).
  • Lamb is ready only when a fork goes in easily. If hard, cook longer with a little water.
  • Rice: after adding water, boil 2 min, then cover and LOWEST heat, no stirring, no opening.
  • Taste the food before serving. Salt is added in small steps.
Tikman bago ihain. Asin paunti-unti.

🧼 Hygiene & safety

  • Wash hands before cooking and after touching raw chicken or meat.
  • Separate board and knife for raw meat and for vegetables.
  • Cool food before closing any lunch box. Sauces in a separate small box.
  • Leftovers: into the fridge within 1 hour, in a closed container, labelled with the day.

📱 Videos (QR codes)

  • Every recipe has a QR code at the bottom. Open the camera on the phone, point at the code, tap the link.
  • Green label = Tagalog video. Blue label = English video.
  • Watch the video ONCE fully before cooking the dish for the first time. Then follow the written recipe in this book for quantities.
  • If something is unclear, ask BEFORE cooking, not after.

Chicken Biryani

برياني دجاج

Layered basmati rice with marinated chicken, fried onions and saffron. Cooked on 'dum' (sealed pot, low heat) so the steam finishes the rice.

⏱ 1 h 15 minLevel: Medium

Ingredients

Ingredient1 person2 persons
Chicken (thigh/leg, bone-in)300 g (2 pcs)600 g (4 pcs)
Basmati rice, washed, soaked 30 min1 cup (200 g)2 cups (400 g)
Plain yogurt3 tbsp6 tbsp
Onion, thinly sliced1 medium2 medium
Tomato, chopped1 small1 large
Ginger-garlic paste1 tsp2 tsp
Biryani masala1 tbsp2 tbsp
Turmeric1/4 tsp1/2 tsp
Kashmiri red chili powder (mild)1/2 tsp1 tsp
Whole spices: cinnamon stick, 3 cardamom, 2 cloves, 1 bay leaf1 set1 set
Fresh mint + coriander, chopped2 tbsp4 tbsp
Saffron in 2 tbsp warm milksmall pinchbig pinch
Ghee or oil2 tbsp4 tbsp
Saltto tasteto taste

Method

  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic, biryani masala, turmeric, chili, salt. Minimum 30 min (overnight is best).
  2. Fry sliced onion in ghee on medium heat until deep golden and crispy (10-12 min). Remove half for topping.
  3. Add marinated chicken to the pot with the remaining onion. Cook 10 min until chicken is sealed and sauce thick. Add tomato, cook 5 min.
  4. In another pot boil 6 cups water with salt and whole spices. Add soaked rice. Boil 5-6 min until rice is 70 percent cooked (still firm in center). Drain.
  5. Layer: chicken at bottom, rice on top, then fried onion, mint, coriander and saffron milk.
  6. Cover pot with a clean cloth then the lid (seal). Cook LOW heat 20 min. Turn off. Rest 10 min without opening.
  7. Serve from the side with a flat spoon so layers stay. Serve with raita (yogurt + cucumber).
Mahalagang paalala (Tagalog)
  • Rice ay 70% lang luto bago i-layer. Kung lutong-luto na, magiging lata (mushy).
  • Huwag buksan ang takip sa 20 min na dum. Pinakamababang apoy.
  • Sibuyas dapat golden brown talaga, hindi sunog (mapait).

Lamb Biryani

Same method as chicken biryani but the lamb is cooked first until tender. Lamb needs more time than chicken; never rush it.

Chicken Maqluba

مقلوبة دجاج

Palestinian 'upside-down' rice. Fried eggplant, cauliflower and potato are layered with chicken and rice, then the whole pot is flipped onto a tray.

⏱ 1 h 30 minLevel: Medium

Ingredients

Ingredient1 person2 persons
Chicken pieces, bone-in350 g700 g
Basmati rice, washed, soaked 20 min1 cup2 cups
Eggplant, sliced 1 cm1/2 medium1 medium
Cauliflower florets1 cup2 cups
Potato, sliced 1 cm1 small1 large
Tomato, sliced12
Onion, chopped1/21
Maqluba / 7-spice (baharat)1 tsp2 tsp
Turmeric1/4 tsp1/2 tsp
Cinnamon stick, 2 cardamom, 1 bay leaf1 set1 set
Chicken stock (from boiling chicken)1.5 cups3 cups
Oil for fryingas neededas needed
Toasted almonds / pine nuts for topping1 tbsp2 tbsp
Salt + black pepperto tasteto taste

Method

  1. Boil chicken with onion, whole spices and salt for 25 min. Keep the stock. Brown the chicken pieces in a little oil.
  2. Salt eggplant slices 10 min, pat dry. Fry eggplant, cauliflower and potato separately until golden. Drain on paper.
  3. Mix drained rice with baharat, turmeric, salt.
  4. In a deep pot, put a layer of tomato slices at the bottom, then chicken, then fried vegetables, then rice on top. Press gently.
  5. Pour hot stock slowly down the side until it just covers the rice. Bring to boil, then cover and cook LOW 35-40 min until liquid is absorbed.
  6. Turn off, rest 10 min. Put a large tray on top of the pot and flip it over in one quick move. Wait 2 min, lift the pot.
  7. Top with toasted nuts and parsley. Serve with yogurt and salad.

Yellow Rice with Peri-Peri Chicken (Nando's style)

أرز أصفر مع دجاج بيري بيري

Grilled chicken in a tangy peri-peri marinade with turmeric yellow rice. Use MILD peri-peri for Einas; medium for Mohamed.

⏱ 1 h (plus marinating)Level: Easy

Lamb Kabsa

كبسة لحم

The classic Gulf rice. Lamb is simmered with tomato, dried lime (loomi) and kabsa spices; the rice cooks in the same stock.

⏱ 2 hLevel: Medium

Ingredients

Ingredient1 person2 persons
Lamb with bone350 g700 g
Basmati rice, washed, soaked 20 min1 cup2 cups
Onion, chopped12
Tomato, grated or chopped1 large2 large
Tomato paste1 tbsp2 tbsp
Garlic, minced2 cloves4 cloves
Kabsa spice mix1 tbsp2 tbsp
Dried lime (loomi), pierced12
Cinnamon stick, 3 cardamom, 2 cloves, 1 bay leaf1 set1 set
Carrot, grated (optional)1/21
Ghee / oil2 tbsp4 tbsp
Water2.5 cups5 cups
Raisins + toasted almonds for topping1 tbsp each2 tbsp each
Saltto tasteto taste

Method

  1. Fry onion in ghee until golden. Add garlic, lamb. Brown 5 min.
  2. Add tomato, tomato paste, kabsa spice, loomi, whole spices, salt. Stir 3 min.
  3. Add water. Boil, skim foam, cover, simmer 60-75 min until lamb is tender.
  4. Take out lamb pieces, keep warm. Measure the stock: you need 1.5 cups stock per 1 cup rice (add water if less).
  5. Add rice and carrot to the stock. Boil 2 min, cover, LOW 18-20 min. Rest 5 min.
  6. Optional: put lamb under the oven grill 5 min for colour. Serve lamb on rice, top with raisins and almonds. Serve with daqoos (tomato chili sauce) and salad.
Mahalagang paalala (Tagalog)
  • Sukatin ang stock: 1.5 cups stock = 1 cup rice. Ito ang sikreto ng magandang kabsa.
