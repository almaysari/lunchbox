Chicken Biryani

Real photos of Chicken Biryani. The finished dish should look like this: separate grains, golden chicken on top, fried onion, almonds and raisins.
Source uses 1/4 tsp crushed chili flakes and 2 chopped green chilies and suggests daqqous chili sauce for serving; all three are removed and replaced with 1/4 tsp sweet paprika for colour and a squeeze of lemon at the table.

Part 1 - Spiced Chicken in Sauce

Ingredients (1 adult)

Chicken thighs and drumsticks, bone-in, skinless (pat dry with kitchen paper; do not wash)200 g (2 pcs)
Ghee or butter1/2 tbsp
Ground cardamom1/8 tsp
Black pepper, ground1/8 tsp
Sweet paprika (replaces the chili flakes of the source)1/4 tsp
Turmeric1/8 tsp
Ground cumin1/8 tsp
Salt1/3 tsp (a little less than 1/2)
Onion, finely chopped1/2 medium (60 g)
Garlic, crushed1 clove
Fresh ginger, grated1/2 tsp
Tomatoes, peeled and chopped1/2 large (75 g)
Plain yogurt1 1/2 tbsp
Bay leaf1 pcs
Cinnamon stick1 pcs (half stick, 4 cm)
Fresh mint, chopped1/2 tbsp
Fresh coriander, chopped1 tbsp
Water40 ml (1/6 cup)

Before cooking

  • Pat the 2 chicken pieces dry with kitchen paper on the raw-chicken board. Do not wash the chicken.
  • Chop 1/2 medium onion finely, crush 2 garlic cloves, grate 1/2 tsp ginger on the vegetable board.
  • Score a cross on 1/2 large tomato, drop it in boiling water for 30 seconds, peel and chop it.
  • Chop 1/2 tbsp mint and 1 tbsp coriander. Measure all the ground spices into one small bowl.

Steps

  1. Melt 1/2 tbsp ghee in a large non-stick skillet 28 cm over medium heat for 1 minute. Add the spice bowl (1/8 tsp cardamom, 1/8 tsp black pepper, 1/4 tsp paprika, 1/8 tsp turmeric, 1/8 tsp cumin, 1/3 tsp (a little less than 1/2) salt) and stir for 20 seconds until the spices smell toasted but are not dark.
  2. Add the 1 chopped onion, 1 crushed garlic clove and 1/2 tsp grated ginger to the skillet, still on medium heat, and stir for 6 to 8 minutes until the onion is soft, light golden and smells sweet.
  3. Add the 200 g chicken pieces to the skillet on medium heat and turn them with tongs for 4 minutes until every piece is coated in the yellow onion mixture and the outside is no longer pink.
  4. Add the 1 chopped tomato, 1 1/2 tbsp yogurt, 1 bay leaf, the half cinnamon stick, 1/2 tbsp mint and 1 tbsp coriander. Stir once, cover the skillet and cook on medium-low heat for 10 minutes until the tomato has collapsed into a sauce.
  5. Pour in 40 ml water, cover again and simmer on low heat for 30 minutes, turning the chicken once at 15 minutes, until the chicken reads 74 C on a thermometer at the thickest part and only about 1/4 cup of thick sauce remains. Turn off the heat and keep covered.

Part 2 - Saffron Basmati Rice and Assembly

Ingredients (1 adult)

Basmati rice1/2 cup (100 g)
Water for cooking the rice2/3 cup (160 ml)
Rose water3/4 tbsp
Saffron threads1/8 tsp
Ghee1/2 tbsp
Almonds, blanched1 tbsp
Raisins1 tbsp
Onion, chopped1/2 small (40 g)
Fresh ginger, grated1/8 tsp
Whole cardamom pods1 pcs
Whole cloves1 pcs
Cinnamon stick1 pcs (half stick, 4 cm)
Salt1/3 tsp (a little less than 1/2)

Before cooking

  • Put 1/2 cup basmati rice in a bowl, cover with cold water, swirl with your hand and pour the water off. Repeat 4 to 5 times until the water runs almost clear. Cover with fresh cold water and soak for 30 minutes, then drain in a sieve.
  • Mix 3/4 tbsp rose water and 1/8 tsp saffron in a small cup and leave it to colour while the rice soaks.
  • Chop 1/2 small onion.

Steps

  1. Heat 1/2 tbsp ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot 20 cm over medium heat for 1 minute. Add 1 tbsp almonds and stir for 2 to 3 minutes until light golden, then lift them out with a slotted spoon onto a plate. Add 1 tbsp raisins to the same ghee and stir for 30 to 60 seconds until they puff up, then lift them out to the same plate.
  2. In the same pot on medium heat add the 1 chopped small onion, 1/8 tsp grated ginger, 2 cardamom pods, 1 clove and the half cinnamon stick. Stir for 2 minutes until the onion is soft and the whole spices smell fragrant.
  3. Add the drained 1/2 cup rice and 1/3 tsp (a little less than 1/2) salt to the pot and stir gently with a wooden spoon for 1 minute so every grain is coated in ghee and spice.
  4. Pour in 2/3 cup water, raise the heat to high and boil uncovered for 5 to 7 minutes until most of the water has gone and small holes appear on the surface of the rice.
  5. Lay the 2 cooked chicken pieces on top of the rice and pour the remaining sauce from the skillet over them. Drizzle the saffron rose water from the cup over everything. Cover the pot with a clean kitchen towel and then the lid, and cook on the lowest heat for 25 minutes until the rice grains are long, dry and separate and no liquid is left at the bottom (tilt the pot to check).
  6. Turn off the heat and leave the pot covered for 5 minutes. Lift the chicken off onto a plate, fluff the rice with a fork from the sides inward, and turn it onto a wide serving dish.
Red daqoos sauce served next to the rice, as in a Gulf madhbi tray.

Part 3 - Red Sauce (Daqoos, non-spicy) - صلصة دقوس بدون حار

Gulf tomato sauce served beside biryani and kabsa. The traditional daqoos has hot chili; this version has NONE. Makes about 1/2 cup, enough for 1 adult. Prep 5 min, cook 20 min.

Ingredients (1 adult)

Ripe tomatoes, chopped1 1/2 medium (200 g)
Tomato paste1/2 tbsp
Garlic, crushed1 clove
Onion, finely chopped1/4 small (20 g)
Olive oil1/2 tbsp
Ground cumin1/4 tsp
Ground coriander1/8 tsp
Sweet paprika (for colour, replaces chili)1/4 tsp
Sugar1/8 tsp
Salt1/4 tsp
Lemon juice1/2 tsp
Water30 ml (1/8 cup)
Fresh coriander, chopped1/2 tbsp

Before cooking

  • Chop 1 1/2 tomatoes into small cubes, chop 1/4 small onion finely, crush 2 garlic cloves, chop 1/2 tbsp coriander.
  • Use the vegetable board, not the chicken board.

Steps

  1. Heat 1/2 tbsp olive oil in a small saucepan over medium heat for 1 minute. Add the 1/2 chopped onion and 1 crushed garlic clove and stir for 3 minutes until soft and lightly golden, not brown.
  2. Add 1/2 tbsp tomato paste and stir for 1 minute until it darkens slightly and smells sweet.
  3. Add the 200 g chopped tomatoes, 30 ml water, 1/4 tsp cumin, 1/8 tsp coriander, 1/4 tsp sweet paprika, 1/8 tsp sugar and 1/4 tsp salt. Stir, bring to a boil, then lower to low heat and simmer uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes, until the tomatoes collapse and the sauce is thick enough to coat a spoon.
  4. Turn off the heat. Blend with a hand blender for 20 seconds until smooth (or leave it chunky if preferred). Stir in 1/2 tsp lemon juice and 1/2 tbsp chopped coriander. Taste: it should be tangy and tomato-rich, with NO heat.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature in a small bowl next to the biryani. It keeps in a closed container in the fridge for 3 days.

Tagalog

  • WALANG sili sa sauce na ito. Kamatis, bawang, sibuyas, kumin at paprika lang.
  • Pakuluan ng mahina 15 minuto hanggang lumapot.
  • Ihain sa maliit na mangkok sa tabi ng biryani.
Arabic chopped salad: tomato, cucumber, parsley, lemon and olive oil.

Part 4 - Arabic Salad (Salata Arabiya) - سلطة عربية

Fresh chopped salad, 1 adult. Prep 10 min, no cooking. Make it last, 10 minutes before serving.

Ingredients (1 adult)

Tomato, firm, small cubes1 medium (120 g)
Cucumber, small cubes (peel if skin is thick)1/2 (80 g)
Onion, very finely chopped1 tbsp (15 g)
Fresh parsley, finely chopped1 tbsp
Fresh mint, finely chopped (optional)1 tsp
Lemon juice1 tbsp
Olive oil1 tbsp
Salt1/4 tsp
Sumac (optional)1/4 tsp

Steps

  1. Wash the tomato, cucumber, parsley and mint under running water. Dry the herbs well with kitchen paper (wet herbs make the salad watery).
  2. Cut 1 tomato and 1/2 cucumber into small cubes about 1 cm. Chop 1 tbsp onion very finely. Chop 1 tbsp parsley and 1 tsp mint finely with a sharp knife.
  3. Put everything in a small bowl. In a cup, mix 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1/4 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp sumac.
  4. Pour the dressing over the salad only at serving time and toss gently once. Serve in a small bowl next to the biryani. No chili.

Tagalog

  • Hiwain nang maliliit at pantay (1 cm).
  • Dressing (lemon + olive oil + asin) ilagay BAGO ihain lang, para hindi malata.
  • Walang sili.

Cooking timeline

  1. 0:00 Wash the rice and start the 30 minute soak; mix saffron and rose water.
  2. 0:05 Prepare onion, garlic, ginger, tomato, herbs and the spice bowl.
  3. 0:15 Start Part 1: fry spices and onion, add chicken, then tomato and yogurt (10 min covered).
  4. 0:35 Add water to the chicken and simmer 30 minutes.
  5. 0:40 Fry almonds and raisins, then onion and whole spices in the rice pot.
  6. 0:45 Add rice and water, boil until the water is almost absorbed.
  7. 1:05 Chicken is done: place it on the rice with its sauce, add saffron water, steam on lowest heat 25 minutes.
  8. 1:30 Rest 5 minutes, fluff and serve.
  9. Last 10 minutes while the rice steams: make the Arabic salad.
  10. While the chicken simmers (step 5 of Part 1), make the red sauce: 20 minutes, it waits happily off the heat.

Common mistakes

  • Rice turns mushy -> use the exact 2/3 cup water for 1/2 cup rice and boil off the water before the steaming stage; do not add extra.
  • Chicken dry or stringy -> keep the heat low once the water is in and stop at 74 C; thighs and drumsticks forgive better than breast.
  • Spices burn in step 1 -> stir for 20 seconds only and have the onion ready to add immediately.
  • Bottom of the rice scorches -> use the lowest flame and a heavy pot; a heat diffuser helps on gas.
  • Yogurt splits into grains -> add it off a hard boil on medium-low heat and stir it in gently.

Serving

Turn the rice onto a wide oval platter and arrange the 2 chicken pieces on top. Scatter the fried almonds and raisins over the chicken. Serve with a bowl of plain yogurt and a simple cucumber and tomato salad, and lemon wedges instead of the daqqous chili sauce.

Food safety

  • Chicken is safe at 74 C internal temperature at the thickest part next to the bone.
  • Do not wash raw chicken; use a separate board and knife for the raw chicken and wash hands after handling it.
  • Cool leftovers within 1 hour, keep in a closed container in the fridge for 2 to 3 days.
  • Reheat rice and chicken until steaming hot all the way through; reheat rice only once.

Mahalagang paalala (Tagalog)

  • Rice: 70% luto lang bago i-layer. Kung lutong-luto, magiging lata.
  • HUWAG buksan ang takip habang nag-steam (dum). Pinakamababang apoy.
  • Sibuyas dapat golden brown, HINDI sunog (mapait).
  • WALANG sili, walang chili flakes, walang hot sauce. Hindi maanghang.
  • Manok luto kapag 74 C sa thermometer o malinaw ang katas, walang pink sa buto.
  • Huwag hugasan ang hilaw na manok. Hiwalay na board para sa manok at gulay.
